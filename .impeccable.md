## Design Context

### Users

Moyra is used by Hugo Montano's legal practice, internal attorneys, pasantes, administrative operators, and external clients. The `Casos` module supports confidential legal communication: clients sign in, review assigned cases, read private updates, comment, upload documents, and manage notifications. Internal users manage cases, statuses, members, file visibility, and audit trails.

### Brand Personality

Restrained, precise, and trustworthy. The interface should feel like a serious legal operations system: clear, calm, and direct, with confidence coming from structure and legibility rather than decoration.

### Aesthetic Direction

Use a work-focused, rectilinear Moyra UI with dense but readable information hierarchy. Prefer light, operational screens that support repeated scanning and case work. Avoid marketing-style hero sections, decorative card-heavy layouts, generic AI gradients, excessive glassmorphism, and playful visual motifs. Keep public Publications visually unchanged unless explicitly scoped.

### Design Principles

- Confidentiality first: private case content must never look or behave like public publication content.
- Operational density: prioritize scannable tables, timelines, forms, and status controls over decorative layout.
- Clear permission boundaries: client, pasante, attorney, and admin capabilities should be visually and behaviorally distinct.
- Stable controls: fixed chrome, forms, tables, document lists, and notification controls should not overlap or shift across desktop and mobile.
- Release restraint: SES email and Web Push should remain visually present only where configured and should use safe summaries, not confidential legal content.
